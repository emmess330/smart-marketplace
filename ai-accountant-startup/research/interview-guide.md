# Accountant User Research Interview Guide

A comprehensive interview guide for validating the AI accounting assistant concept with target users.

## Interview Objectives

**Primary Goals:**
1. Understand daily workflows and pain points
2. Identify highest-value use cases for automation
3. Validate willingness to pay for AI solution
4. Discover objections and concerns
5. Learn about current tools and workarounds

**Target**: 30-50 interviews
- 15-20 CPAs/accountants (small firms)
- 10-15 Bookkeepers (independent/small practices)
- 5-10 Small business owners (self-manage accounting)

---

### Interview Logistics

- **Duration**: 30-45 minutes (tell them 15-30 min to reduce friction)

## Interview Structure

### Timing Breakdown (45 min total)

1. **Intro & Rapport** (5 min)
2. **Background & Context** (5 min)
3. **Workflow Deep Dive** (15 min)
4. **Pain Points & Challenges** (10 min)
5. **Solution Validation** (5 min)
6. **Willingness to Pay** (3 min)
7. **Wrap-up** (2 min)

---

## Interview Script

### 1. Introduction & Rapport (5 min)

**Your Intro:**
```
Thanks so much for taking the time! I'm [Name], and I'm researching 
how accountants work day-to-day. I'm exploring building software to 
help automate some of the repetitive tasks.

This is pure research - no product to sell you today. I want to understand 
your workflow, challenges, and what would actually be useful.

Sound good? Great, let's dive in!

[Get consent to record if applicable]
```

**Warm-up Questions:**
- How's your day going so far?
- Where are you based?
- How long have you been in accounting?

---

### 2. Background & Context (5 min)

**Goal**: Understand their role, firm size, client base

**Questions:**

1. **Tell me about your current role.**
   - What's your title?
   - How long have you been in this role?
   - What's your firm size? (solo, 2-10, 10+)

2. **Walk me through your typical client base.**
   - How many active clients do you have?
   - What types of businesses? (freelancers, small biz, etc.)
   - What industries? (tech, retail, services, etc.)

3. **What services do you provide?**
   - [ ] Bookkeeping
   - [ ] Tax preparation
   - [ ] Financial advisory
   - [ ] Payroll
   - [ ] Audit support
   - [ ] Other: ___________

4. **How seasonal is your work?**
   - What's your busiest time of year?
   - How many hours/week during peak? Off-peak?

**Listen for:**
- Firm size (solo vs team dynamics)
- Client complexity (simple vs complex needs)
- Revenue model (hourly vs fixed vs retainer)
- Workload intensity (burnout risk?)

---

### 3. Workflow Deep Dive (15 min)

**Goal**: Map their entire workflow to identify automation opportunities

**Questions:**

5. **Walk me through a typical day or week.**
   - What time do you start working?
   - What are you doing first thing?
   - What tasks fill most of your time?
   - What's the last thing you do before wrapping up?

6. **Let's dig into bookkeeping specifically. How does that process work?**
   - How do clients send you receipts/invoices? (email, dropbox, mail?)
   - What do you do when you receive them?
   - What software do you use? (QuickBooks, Xero, Excel, etc.)
   - How long does it take to process 50 receipts?

7. **Tell me about your bank reconciliation process.**
   - How often do you reconcile accounts?
   - Do you connect banks directly or import CSVs?
   - How do you categorize transactions?
   - What's the hardest part of reconciliation?

8. **Now let's talk about tax season. Walk me through your typical tax prep workflow.**
   - When do you start preparing for tax season?
   - What information do you need from clients?
   - How do you organize that info?
   - What's your process for filling out tax forms?
   - What tax software do you use? (TurboTax, TaxAct, Drake, Lacerte?)
   - How long does a typical tax return take? (1040, 1120, etc.)

9. **What about financial reporting?**
   - What reports do clients ask for most? (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow?)
   - How do you generate those reports?
   - Do you add commentary/analysis, or just send the report?
   - How often do clients want reports?

10. **Let's talk about client communication.**
    - How do clients reach you? (email, phone, portal, text?)
    - What questions do they ask most frequently?
    - How much time do you spend answering client questions?
    - Do clients understand financial concepts, or do you have to explain a lot?

**Listen for:**
- Manual, repetitive tasks (data entry, categorization)
- Time-consuming steps (receipt processing, form filling)
- Error-prone processes (manual entry, calculations)
- Friction points (client communication, chasing documents)

---

### 4. Pain Points & Challenges (10 min)

**Goal**: Identify the biggest frustrations and unmet needs

**Questions:**

11. **What's the most frustrating part of your job?**
    - Why is that frustrating?
    - How often does this happen?
    - How does it impact your work?

12. **If you could wave a magic wand and eliminate one task from your workflow, what would it be?**
    - Why that task specifically?
    - How much time would that save you per week?
    - What would you do with that time?

13. **Tell me about the last time you made a mistake or had to redo work.**
    - What happened?
    - What caused the mistake?
    - How did you catch it?
    - How long did it take to fix?

14. **What's the hardest part about scaling your practice?**
    - What stops you from taking on more clients?
    - Is it time, team, processes, or something else?
    - Have you tried hiring? How did that go?

15. **What keeps you up at night about your work?**
    - Compliance/regulatory concerns?
    - Client satisfaction?
    - Workload/burnout?
    - Technology/security?

16. **What tools do you currently use? (Let them list all software)**
    - Accounting software: _________
    - Tax software: _________
    - Document management: _________
    - Communication: _________
    - Other: _________
    
17. **What do you love about your current tools?**
    - What works really well?
    - What would you never give up?

18. **What frustrates you about your current tools?**
    - What doesn't work well?
    - What's missing?
    - What takes too long?

**Listen for:**
- Emotional language ("hate", "dread", "painful")
- Time estimates (quantify pain)
- Workarounds (signs of unmet needs)
- Aspirations ("I wish I could...")

---

### 5. Solution Validation (5 min)

**Goal**: Test concepts without leading the witness

**Setup:**
```
Based on what you've shared, I'm exploring a few ideas. I'll describe 
them briefly, and I'd love your honest reaction - both positive and 
negative. Sound good?
```

**Present Concepts (One at a Time):**

**Concept 1: AI Receipt Scanner**
```
Imagine you could just snap a photo of a receipt with your phone, 
and it automatically extracted the vendor, amount, date, and category. 
It would learn your categorization patterns over time.

What's your reaction to that?
```

**Follow-up:**
- Would you actually use that?
- What would make you NOT use it?
- What's missing from that description?
- How much would you pay monthly for that feature alone?

---

**Concept 2: Conversational AI for Reports**
```
Imagine you could ask questions in plain English, like "What's my 
client's profit margin for Q3?" and instantly get a visual report 
with charts. You could ask follow-ups like "How does that compare 
to last year?"

What do you think about that?
```

**Follow-up:**
- Would you use that with clients? For yourself?
- What questions would you ask?
- Would clients find this useful, or confusing?
- Any concerns about accuracy?

---

**Concept 3: AI Tax Assistant**
```
Imagine software that analyzed your client's transactions throughout 
the year and automatically flagged potential deductions, estimated 
quarterly tax payments, and suggested tax-saving strategies. It 
could even pre-fill common tax forms.

How would you feel about that?
```

**Follow-up:**
- Would you trust it, or double-check everything?
- What would make you trust it?
- What's your biggest concern?
- Would your clients want this direct, or prefer you in the middle?

---

**Open Question:**
19. **If I could build you the perfect accounting tool, what would it do?**
    - What's the #1 feature?
    - What else?
    - What should it NOT do?

**Listen for:**
- Immediate enthusiasm (or skepticism)
- Specific use cases they mention
- Objections (trust, accuracy, complexity)
- Price sensitivity signals

---

### 6. Willingness to Pay (3 min)

**Goal**: Gauge price sensitivity and budget

**Questions:**

20. **How much do you currently spend on accounting software per month?**
    - QuickBooks: $___
    - Tax software: $___
    - Other tools: $___
    - Total: $___

21. **If a tool could save you 10 hours per week, what would that be worth to you monthly?**
    - (Let them answer first)
    - Would $50/month be reasonable?
    - What about $100/month?
    - At what price would it be too expensive?

22. **Would you rather pay per transaction, per client, or flat monthly fee?**
    - Why that model?
    - What's your preference?

**Listen for:**
- Budget constraints (bootstrapped vs funded)
- Value framing (time savings vs revenue)
- Pricing model preference

---

### 7. Wrap-up (2 min)

**Questions:**

23. **Is there anything I didn't ask that I should have?**

24. **If I build this, would you be interested in testing an early version?**
    - Can I follow up with you in a few months?
    - Can I send you a demo when it's ready?

25. **Do you know 2-3 other accountants who might be willing to chat with me?**
    - (Get names and intros if possible)

**Closing:**
```
This was incredibly helpful - thank you so much for your time and 
insights. I'll send you that $50 Amazon gift card within 24 hours.

And if I build this, you'll be first to know!

Thanks again!
```

## Red Flags to Watch For

**Bad signs:**
- 🚩 "That sounds cool" but can't name specific use case
- 🚩 Long pauses when asked about pain points (maybe it's not that painful?)
- 🚩 "I'd use it if it was free" (price sensitivity)
- 🚩 "I'd have to check with my firm" (not the decision maker)
- 🚩 Can't think of anyone to refer you to (not engaged in community)

**Good signs:**
- ✅ Shares detailed workflow without prompting
- ✅ Gets excited describing pain points
- ✅ Asks "when can I get this?"
- ✅ Offers to beta test
- ✅ Gives you 3+ referrals immediately

---

## Iteration Guide

**After 5 interviews:**
- Refine questions based on what's working
- Drop questions that don't yield insights
- Add follow-ups for unexpected themes

**After 10 interviews:**
- Do you have clear patterns? (If no, keep going)
- Have you identified top 3 pain points? (If no, probe deeper)
- Do you understand willingness to pay? (If no, test more price points)

**After 20 interviews:**
- Strong conviction about problem? (If no, pivot focus)
- Clear feature priorities? (If no, test more concepts)
- Validated price range? (If no, test packaging/tiers)

**When to stop:**
- ✅ You're hearing the same things over and over
- ✅ You can predict what they'll say
- ✅ You have conviction about what to build
- ✅ You know exactly who will pay and how much

## Next Steps After Interviews

### 1. Synthesize Findings (1-2 days)

Create a research report:

**Executive Summary**
- Key insights (top 3)
- Validated pain points
- Top feature priorities
- Pricing recommendation

**Detailed Findings**
- Workflow map (visual diagram)
- Pain point analysis (quotes, frequency, severity)
- Feature validation results
- Competitive tool analysis
- Willingness to pay analysis

**Recommendations**
- MVP feature set (must-haves)
- Nice-to-haves (v2+)
- Pricing strategy
- Target customer profile

### 2. Build Low-Fi Prototype (1 week)

Based on top feature requests:
- Figma mockups
- Clickable prototype
- 5-minute demo video

### 3. Validation Round 2 (2 weeks)

Go back to 10 interviewees:
- Show prototype
- Get feedback
- Test willingness to pay with concrete product
- Ask for pre-orders ($50 early access discount)

### 4. Build MVP (4-8 weeks)

Focus on:
- Top 1-2 features only
- Manual workarounds for complex features
- Get it in users' hands FAST
